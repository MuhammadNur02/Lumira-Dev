## `EPERM` errors on Windows

Move the project out of OneDrive or Dropbox, delete `node_modules` and `.next`, and reinstall.

## "Activation limit reached"

Free an activation from [Licenses](/account/licenses) or run `npx lumira@latest deactivate` in a project you no longer use.

## Webhooks return 401

The signing secret in your provider's dashboard must match `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET` exactly.

## My download link expired

Email links work for 72 hours and up to 5 downloads. After that, download any version from your [Library](/account/library).
