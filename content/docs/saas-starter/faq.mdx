## Can I build a paid SaaS with a Personal license?

A Personal license covers one end product that you do not sell to end users. Paid SaaS products need an Extended license.

## Do I get new major versions?

Your license includes every release within the major version you bought. New majors are offered as a discounted upgrade
from your Library.

## Where do I get help?

Verified owners can join the Lumira Discord from [Support](/account/support). Email support@lumira.dev any time.
