---
title: Editing content
description: Where Docsmith's content lives and how to change it without touching components.
product: docsmith
since: 1.0.0
---

Docsmith keeps content separate from layout: Fumadocs MDX with `meta.json` ordering. Change the text and media there and the pages update on the next build.

- Images go in `public/` or a remote host allowed in `next.config.ts`; always set `alt` text.
- Colors, radii and fonts come from the CSS variables in `app/globals.css`.
- Dark mode is designed, not inverted: check both themes after changing a color.
